Frequently asked questions

Can I rent equipment on the day?

Same-day rentals may be available. Popular sizes can run out on weekends and during peak season, so please contact us in advance.

Can I exchange an item if it doesn't fit?

Yes. If an item does not fit, we can exchange it at the shop. Please ask about availability in your size.

Can you deliver equipment near the slopes?

Delivery and return near the slopes may be available. Please contact us to confirm the location, time and conditions.

Are gloves and socks available to rent?

For hygiene reasons, gloves and socks are sold at the shop rather than rented.

What are the cancellation and equipment damage policies?

Please ask us for the applicable cancellation, refund and damage terms before confirming your reservation.

How do I pay?

Please ask us to confirm the payment method and any payment required for your booking.
